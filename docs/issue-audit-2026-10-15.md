## Reconciliation rules

`Done` requires an actually completed scope; it is not inferred from an old board label. Remaining leaf deliveries are `Blocked` only for actual open native prerequisites, otherwise `Ready` for their remaining scope. Unfinished feature parents remain `Backlog`. Existing Project fields/views and native dependency edges are retained; the native and legacy Status fields are reconciled without creating another board.

Old blocked source-reader, publication or checkout failures may be superseded by a subsequent merged repair and successful current tests. Conversely, no code for a real PTY host, general recommendation approval UI, universal connector capability gate or revisioned model allow-list was found merely because their foundations exist.

The factory was paused during this audit and no new objectives or paid assignments were launched. Its private retained attempts and approvals remain unchanged. Before resuming, reconcile actual PR/main state using the audit evidence rather than replaying already-completed implementation. See [operating MALT on itself](operating-malt-on-itself.md).
