# Community code of conduct

MALT welcomes participation regardless of experience, background, identity or disability. Be considerate of contributors' time and explain technical disagreements with evidence.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, accessible and constructive in issues, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.
- Critique the work and its consequences rather than the person. Accept corrections and give useful context.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing and explicit boundaries. Obtain consent before sharing personal information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and distinguish verified results from speculation or generated claims.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, hate speech, deliberate misgendering, doxxing, impersonation, spam and repeated disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to automated agents and to people representing MALT in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For platform abuse or immediate privacy concerns, use [GitHub's private abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For project conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. If no such channel is available, open an issue requesting **only** a private conduct contact; do not identify involved people or publish sensitive evidence. A dedicated project conduct mailbox is not yet established. Maintainers must arrange a private channel before requesting details. Anyone unable to contact an uninvolved maintainer may use GitHub's reporting route for platform-policy violations.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly, share details only as necessary, and recuse themselves from decisions involving their own conduct. Outcomes can include a private correction, warning, content moderation, a temporary restriction or a permanent participation ban proportionate to severity and repetition. Urgent harmful content may be restricted before review. When safe, explain the decision and allow an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer; do not retaliate against good-faith reports.

This project-specific policy does not replace GitHub's terms or create an emergency response service. There is currently one listed maintainer, so independent appeals and backup coverage need additional maintainers; see [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).
