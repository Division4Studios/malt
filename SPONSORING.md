# Support MALT

[**Sponsor Division4Studios on GitHub**](https://github.com/sponsors/Division4Studios) · [Roadmap to 1.0](https://github.com/Division4Studios/malt/issues/263) · [Project board](https://github.com/users/Division4Studios/projects/10) · [Discussions](https://github.com/Division4Studios/malt/discussions)

MALT is an MIT-licensed effort to make local multi-agent development understandable, resource-aware and accountable. Sponsorship helps the maintainer spend more time building, reviewing, documenting and validating it.

## What funding supports

- Subscription access and appropriate development resources for testing Codex/Claude workflows against real supported interfaces. The factory's subscription-only billing boundary remains unchanged.
- Windows, Linux and macOS verification, owned-process/terminal reliability, security triage and reproducible regression fixtures.
- Usability, accessible operator feedback, clear documentation, focused lifecycle skills and contributor support.
- Deliberate progress on tracked connector, model-policy and delivery-management scopes rather than unreviewable feature dumps.

Sponsorship supports the overall open-source work; it is not a purchase of inference credits, guaranteed features, priority merges, exclusive security access or a support SLA. No funds-raised total, allocation percentage or delivery date is implied here. Report vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), regardless of sponsorship.

## Other ways to help

Reproduce a bug, improve a guide, review a small PR, answer a Discussion or share a verified workflow. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); no donation is needed to participate.

## Funding administration

The repository's Sponsor button points to the existing public GitHub Sponsors listing. Current tiers, prices and any benefits are those actually published on GitHub, not promises made by this document. Identity, tax, payout routing and financial terms are controlled by the account owner in the Sponsors dashboard. Public listing visibility alone does not verify payout readiness. Maintainer updates belong in Announcements; accounting details and sponsor personal information stay private.
